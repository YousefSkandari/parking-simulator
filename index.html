About This Simulator

Agent-based simulation of parking policy impacts on the local economy in Ealing, London. Uses real data from Ealing Council including PCN charges (£160/£80 from April 2025), parking bay rates, car park capacities, and estimated business revenues.

Data Sources: Ealing Council, TfL, ONS, Companies House, Ealing BID.

Local multiplier: 1.4x (each £1 on high street generates £1.40 locally).
